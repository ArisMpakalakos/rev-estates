## Platform

web

## Stack

WordPress (user's choice; matches the one-page website in the signed offer, `../Prosfora_Akinita.xlsx`).

## Users

Property owners in Greece who want someone else to run their property as a short-term rental. Other audiences (tenants, buyers, investors) are not confirmed and are out of scope unless the user adds them.

## Product Purpose

Rev Estates is a startup property-management company. Its website presents the company to owners and gives them a way to get in touch (the offer lists a contact form). Success means an owner understands what the company does for them and makes contact.

## Positioning

Undecided. The user will supply the differentiator and the service area. Do not invent either.

## Operating Context

- The offer is a "Digital Presence Package": one-page WordPress site, logo design, graphics (business cards, social templates), domain/hosting/Microsoft 365 email/Google Business setup, plus monthly social media and ad management (Meta/Google).
- Language is Greek. English is not confirmed as a second language.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single page with sections, a contact form, responsive layout, basic SEO (from the offer).
- Service offered: short-term rental management (the user selected only this one). Long-term rental, maintenance and legal/tax services are not confirmed.
- Open decisions: differentiator, service area, section list, pricing visibility, English version.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, client names, property counts, statistics, photography or pricing have been provided. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Speak to an owner handing over a valuable asset: trust and clarity come before persuasion.
2. Make the next step obvious: a visitor should always know how to make contact.
3. State only what the company can truthfully claim as a startup; no invented proof.
4. Greek-first copy and typography; any font must support Greek.
